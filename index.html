About

I am a Research Scientist at the Interdisciplinary Centre for Security, Reliability and Trust (SnT), University of Luxembourg, in the SIGCOM group. Before that I was a postdoctoral researcher at IMT Atlantique in France.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Nantes in 2022 and my Master's degree from ESEN, University of Manouba, in Tunisia. More about my path.
